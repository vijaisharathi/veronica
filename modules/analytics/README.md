# Analytics Domain (`modules/analytics`)

## Overview
Business intelligence, conversion rate tracking, sales reporting, and customer cohort analysis.

## Roadmap Phase
**Phase 20 — E-Commerce Analytics & Business Intelligence**

## Key Responsibilities
- Event tracking (product views, add-to-cart, checkout initiate, purchase).
- Revenue analytics (gross merchandise value, average order value, return rates).
- Inventory turnover reporting and demand forecasting.
