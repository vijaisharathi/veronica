# Ratings & Reviews Domain (`modules/reviews`)

## Overview
Customer feedback, verified buyer product reviews, photo/video uploads, and rating aggregates.

## Roadmap Phase
**Phase 15 — Customer Reviews, Ratings & Social Proof**

## Key Responsibilities
- Verified buyer review submission with fit and size feedback (e.g. "Runs small", "True to size").
- Media upload moderation and automated sentiment analysis.
- Rating aggregation calculations per product and category.
